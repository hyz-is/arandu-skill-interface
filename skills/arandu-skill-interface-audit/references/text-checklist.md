# Text checklist

## Glossary (from `docs/content/glossary.md`)
- [ ] Search views, translations and e-mails for every forbidden synonym; list each hit with `file:line`.
- [ ] The same action keeps its verb from button to toast to status.
- [ ] Panel, landing, e-mails and help use the same names.

## Generic and template text
- [ ] No phrase from `arandu-skill-interface-content/references/generic-phrases.md` (pt-BR and English).
- [ ] No Lorem Ipsum, "Texto aqui", "Usuário Teste", default provider messages, English leftovers in a pt-BR product.
- [ ] Headlines and section titles could not appear unchanged on a competitor's site.

## AI tells (from `arandu-skill-interface-copywriting/references/ai-tells.md`)
- [ ] Contrast reveals ("Não é só… é…"), negation lists, trailing pile-ons, self-answered questions, stock openers.
- [ ] Em dashes in headlines, buttons and short UI strings.
- [ ] Uniform rhythm, tidy closers, wisdom-shaped sentences.

## Microcopy
- [ ] Buttons: verb + object; no "OK", "Enviar", "Clique aqui".
- [ ] Errors: what failed + whether anything was lost + what to do; no codes as the message; no apology filler.
- [ ] Empty states: kind-specific copy and one action.
- [ ] Confirmations name the action and the object; verb buttons.
- [ ] Sentence case; one form of address; pt-BR formats.

## Facts and proof
- [ ] Every number, customer, logo, testimonial, rating, certification and integration is in `PRODUCT.md` "Evidence on hand" or visible in the product.
- [ ] Structured data restates visible facts only.
- [ ] No competitor named in product text without a request.
