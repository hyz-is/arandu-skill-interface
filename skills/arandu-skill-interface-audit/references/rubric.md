# Review rubric (internal)

The eight dimensions and weights come from HYZIS's master refinement prompt. Use them to decide what to fix first and to compare before/after. Never publish a total without the captures that support each line; when a dimension was not observable, write "not assessed" instead of a number.

| Dimension | Weight | Look at |
|---|---|---|
| Visual identity and originality | 15 % | does it belong to this product (subject inventory, signature detail, preset applied with intent)? |
| Finish and visual precision | 15 % | alignment, spacing rhythm, radius and border consistency, browser surfaces themed |
| Niche-specific, coherent content | 15 % | glossary, voice, specificity, no generic phrases |
| Hierarchy and usability | 15 % | one primary action, squint test, task path, states |
| Motion and microinteractions | 10 % | purposeful, budgeted, reduced-motion safe |
| Consistency of design system and glossary | 10 % | tokens used, no drift from DESIGN.md, terms consistent |
| Native quality and responsiveness | 10 % | 390/768/1280 composition, touch targets; Ayra platform limits stated |
| Accessibility and performance | 10 % | checklist results, contrast, LCP/CLS |

Per dimension write: 0–4 (0 absent, 1 major gaps, 2 partial, 3 good with minor issues, 4 excellent) **with the evidence line**. The weighted figure is for the team's comparison of two runs of the same audit, not a public score.
