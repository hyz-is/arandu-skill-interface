# Brazilian market

## Vocabulary: the niche, not the dictionary

| English habit | In Brazilian products |
|---|---|
| invoice | depends on the niche: **nota fiscal** (fiscal document), **cobrança** or **fatura** (a bill) — choose one per product |
| bank slip | **boleto** |
| instant payment | **Pix** (proper noun) |
| bank reconciliation | **conciliação bancária** |
| billing reminders | **régua de cobrança** |
| tax ID | **CPF** (person), **CNPJ** (company) |
| ZIP code | **CEP** |
| chargeback | **chargeback** or **contestação** (cards) |
| onboarding (KYC) | **cadastro**, **validação de identidade** |
| dashboard | **painel** (most B2B), **dashboard** acceptable in tech products |
| workspace / organization | **conta**, **empresa**, **organização** — follow the niche |
| sign in / sign up | **Entrar** / **Criar conta** |

## Formats

- Currency: `R$ 1.234,56` (non-breaking space after `R$`); never `R$1234.56`.
- Dates `25/10/2026`; months in lowercase in running text ("25 de outubro de 2026"); weekdays lowercase ("quarta-feira").
- Times `14:32` or `14h32` (pick one); 24-hour clock.
- Phones `(11) 98765-4321`; CEP `01304-001`; CPF `123.456.789-09`; CNPJ `12.345.678/0001-95`.
- Decimal comma, thousands dot; percentages `12,5%`.

## Regulatory and trust terms that users expect

- **LGPD** (privacy): consent, data subject rights, the DPO contact ("Encarregado de dados"), retention after cancellation.
- **Nota fiscal** (NF-e, NFS-e) for services and goods; "emitir nota" is the verb.
- **Pix**: chave Pix, QR Code, "copia e cola"; **boleto**: linha digitável, vencimento, multa e juros.
- **Open Finance**, **KYC/validação**, **Receita Federal** situação cadastral for CNPJ.
- Company identification on public sites: razão social and CNPJ in the footer are expected in many niches.

## Tone

Brazilian business users expect "você", directness, and courtesy without filler. Avoid literal translations of English marketing idioms ("leve seu negócio ao próximo nível"), anglicisms where a Portuguese term is standard in the niche, and exclamation marks in system messages.
