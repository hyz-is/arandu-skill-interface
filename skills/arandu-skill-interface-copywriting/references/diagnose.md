# Diagnose before writing

Most generated copy fails by imposing the model's taste on someone who already made good choices. The diagnosis decides how much you may change.

| Situation | You were given | Your job |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Blank or slop** | a one-line request, or generic company-speak with no point of view | build the argument from `PRODUCT.md` and the niche profile; freedom on angle, none on facts |
| **B. Real voice present** | a draft with an angle, opinions or a recognisable voice, even if rough | restore it: keep their argument and vocabulary; fix the buried lead, vague lines, AI tells, structure |
| **C. Point edit** | "fix this headline", "tighten this paragraph" | change only what was asked, plus the minimum around it |

Write the diagnosis in one line with evidence: "B — the draft argues against spreadsheets in the founder's voice and uses 'planilha' and 'na mão'."

Reading taste out of the input (B and C):

- **Vocabulary is a choice.** If they write "planilha" and "na mão", do not upgrade to "soluções" and "automatize".
- **Where they spent words is a priority.** Keep the emphasis.
- **What they left out is scope.** Do not add sections they did not ask for.
- **Their angle is the positioning.** Deepen it; do not replace it.

If a writing sample exists, match its sentence length, punctuation habits and openings; the sample beats the default voice.

For C, return only the changed lines with one reason each.
