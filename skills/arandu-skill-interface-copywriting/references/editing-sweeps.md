# Editing in sweeps

Edit existing copy in focused passes, one concern at a time, and keep a short log of what changed and why.

1. **Clarity** — can a newcomer say what the product does after the first screen? Cut jargon, front-load the key word, one idea per sentence.
2. **Voice** — does it sound like the niche profile's voice everywhere? Same address ("você"), same formality.
3. **So what** — after each feature, does the visitor see what it means for them? Add the consequence or cut the line.
4. **Prove it** — every claim has evidence on the page or in `PRODUCT.md`; otherwise soften to what is true or decline it with its unlock.
5. **Specificity** — replace adjectives with numbers, names of real integrations, real steps.
6. **Emotion** — name the visitor's real situation once ("fim do mês com 40 boletos para conferir"); no manufactured urgency.
7. **Zero risk** — near the CTA, answer the risks: trial terms, cancellation, data export, support.

Then the **glossary pass** (every product term is the glossary's) and the **AI-tell pass** (`ai-tells.md`).

**Leave-it-alone test:** for each change, is it measurably better or just different? Revert what you cannot justify — this matters most when a real voice was present.
