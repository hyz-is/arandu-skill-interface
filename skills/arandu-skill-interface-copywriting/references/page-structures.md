# Page structures

Structures are orders of claims, not templates. Start from the argument; use these to check nothing a visitor needs is missing.

## Home / landing (the Arandu landing sequence, measured)

Observed in loccrm.com, locairtag.com, yxy.dev, kuaa.io (18/10/2026): a hero with `aria-labelledby="hero-title"`, then sections that come from the product itself — the flow ("como funciona"), its sources or regions, features shown with the real UI, the panel, solutions or cases, FAQ, a closing CTA band.

1. **Hero** — headline (≤ 2 lines at every width, ~5–9 words), one supporting sentence, the primary action (and at most one secondary), the real product visible. The first screen answers the visitor's first question.
2. **The mechanism** — how it works, in the product's own steps and words.
3. **Capabilities as proof** — 2–4 sections, each one claim with a real UI fragment or a real number; never a grid of six icon cards.
4. **The panel** — the product as the user will see it, with realistic data.
5. **Who it is for / cases** — only real ones; otherwise the situations the product serves, written as situations.
6. **Pricing or the path to it**.
7. **FAQ** — the real objections, answered with facts.
8. **Closing CTA** — the same action label as the hero.
9. **Footer** — razão social and CNPJ when the niche expects it, links by intent.

## Feature page

Problem in the user's words → how this feature solves it (steps) → the screen → limits and requirements stated honestly → related features → CTA.

## Comparison page (only when asked)

Who each option fits, a factual table with dated sources ("observado em 18/10/2026 nas páginas públicas"), what this product does differently, migration path. "Not observed" is never written as "does not have".

## About

Why the product exists (the mechanism, not a mission statement), who builds it, the company's legal identity, how to reach a human, a CTA.

## Headlines

- Say the outcome in the niche's words: "Cobranças por boleto e Pix, com lembretes que cobram por você".
- Patterns that work when true: "[Outcome] sem [pain]", "[Category] para [audience]", a question in the visitor's own words.
- Never: a single word in italics or colour, "Revolucione…", "O futuro de…", "Tudo em um só lugar".

## CTAs

Verb + what they get: "Começar teste de 14 dias", "Emitir minha primeira cobrança", "Falar com um especialista". Not "Saiba mais", "Começar", "Enviar". One label per intent across the page.
