# AI tells in copy

Pattern names adapted from Corey Haines's marketing skills (MIT, see `NOTICE.md`), with Brazilian Portuguese examples written for this repository. A reader who spots one doubts the claims around it.

## Never

| Pattern | Looks like | Write instead |
|---|---|---|
| Contrast reveal | "Não é só um sistema de cobrança. É sua tranquilidade." | state the thing: "Lembretes automáticos cobram o cliente antes do vencimento." |
| Negation list | "Sem planilhas, sem ligações, sem dor de cabeça." | say what does happen: "O cliente recebe o lembrete por e-mail e WhatsApp." |
| Trailing pile-on | "…o extrato que você já tem, sem exportar, sem copiar, sem esforço." | end at the claim |
| Self-answered question / colon reveal | "O resultado? 3x mais rápido." / "O melhor: ele aprende." | just say it (and only if it is true) |
| Stock openers | "No mundo acelerado de hoje", "Seja você X ou Y", "Diga adeus a", "Desbloqueie o poder de" | start with the fact |
| Em dash in short copy | headlines, CTAs, subject lines with "—" | period, comma or colon |

## Ration

- At most one fragment and one list of three per section; most lines plain and declarative.
- Words that hide a fact: "poderoso", "robusto", "intuitivo", "simples", "fácil", "rápido" (without a number), "inteligente". Replace with the fact.
- Uniform rhythm (every sentence the same length), synonym cycling (changing words for variety), abstract subjects ("A eficiência encontra a inovação").

## Second-generation tells (after cleaning)

- Wisdom-shaped sentences ("Cobrar bem é cuidar do cliente.").
- Tidy closers at the end of every section that restate the heading.
- Every section ending with a CTA.

## The self-check

1. Search the draft for the banned patterns above and for `arandu-skill-interface-content/references/generic-phrases.md`.
2. Read every sentence over 20 words; split or cut.
3. Read the page aloud. Where it sounds like a brochure, rewrite from the facts — swapping synonyms creates new tells.
4. Mark missing proof as `[FALTA: <evidence>]` instead of filling it.
